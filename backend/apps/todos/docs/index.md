---
title: Todos
---
Todos is a Getting Things Done list for each person and for the team.

**Private or team.** A private todo is yours alone: nobody else sees it, not even an admin. A team
todo is everyone's to see, edit, complete and reassign. New todos are private unless you choose
team (an admin can change that default). Projects and areas are private or team the same way, and
a todo can only sit in a project everyone who sees the todo can see: a team todo needs a team
project; a private todo can go in a team project or one of your own private projects.

**Statuses.** Inbox is for anything not yet thought through. Process it into a next action (a
physical, visible step), waiting for (blocked on someone), delegated, someday/maybe, or drop it.
Done and dropped todos are kept.

**Today** shows your open work that is starred, due today or overdue, or whose revisit date has
come. Your work is your private todos, team todos assigned to you, and team todos nobody has
taken yet. A revisit date hides a todo from the working lists until that day.

**Repeating todos** come back when you complete them, with the due date moved on. They keep their
visibility and assignee.

**Weekly review** walks your inbox, last week's completions, what is due, projects without a next
action, what you are waiting for, routines, what is coming up and what has gone stale.

**Linked records.** A todo can point at any record (a person, an organization, a deal). The
record's page lists its team todos and your private ones.

When someone leaves, an admin deactivates them; their private todos stay hidden until an admin
transfers them to someone else. Their team todos stay where they are.
