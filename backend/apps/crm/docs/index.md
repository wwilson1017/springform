The CRM tracks **deals**: sales opportunities with a value, a stage, a person and an organization from
the shared directory, an owner, and an expected close date.

## The pipeline

Every deal sits in one stage: **Lead**, **Qualified**, **Proposal**, **Negotiation**, then **Won** or
**Lost**. Drag a card between columns, or select several and move them together. Moving a deal to Won
sets its win probability to 100% and records the day it closed (today, unless you pick an earlier day);
moving it to Lost sets probability to 0% and asks why. Reopening a lost deal clears the reason.

## Archiving and merging

Archive a deal you no longer want to see; a reason is required. Archived deals leave the board, the
dashboard and every total, and **Restore** brings one back. Merging two deals moves the second deal's
notes, files, activity and reminders onto the first and archives the second.

## Activity, notes and Today

Log calls, emails and meetings as activity. Notes, attachments, custom fields and reminders on a deal
work as they do everywhere else. The dashboard's **Today** list shows hot deals that have gone quiet
and your reminders due today; a deal is quiet when nothing has happened on it for the number of days
set in Settings → CRM (14 by default).

## Scores and touch counts

Each deal and person gets a 0–100 **lead score** from the stage, value, activity and how recently
anything happened. When an AI provider is set up, Whisk also counts each deal's **touches** (real
conversations with the prospect) from its notes and activity; without one, the count stays empty.
