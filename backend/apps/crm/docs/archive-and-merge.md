## Archive is not the same as Lost

- **Lost** is a real outcome: the deal happened and you did not win it. It belongs in your win
  rate and your loss reasons.
- **Archived** means "stop showing me this": a duplicate, a test record, a mistake.

Never archive a deal that genuinely closed; mark it won or lost instead.

## Archiving

Archive needs a **reason**. The reason, your name and the time are saved on the deal and appear in
its history, so whoever finds it later knows who put it away and why. Nothing is deleted.

An archived deal leaves the board, the dashboard, the totals, the analytics and the deal lists on
people and organizations. Its activity history stays readable: that is what you read before
deciding to bring it back. Archiving does not count as contact with the customer, so archiving
and restoring a deal does not reset how long it has gone untouched.

While a deal is archived its stage cannot change (a deal both won and archived would book revenue
no report could see), it cannot be moved in bulk, and archiving it again keeps the original date,
person and reason.

## Bringing one back

Show archived deals on the board (or search with archived included), open the deal, and press
**Restore**. It goes straight back on the board.

## Merging two deals

Merging folds one deal (the source) into another (the one you keep). The source's notes,
attachments, activity, reminders and linked to-dos move to the deal you keep; custom fields fill in
only where the kept deal is blank, so a merge never overwrites what you are keeping. The kept
deal's own title, value and stage do not change. The source is archived with the reason
"Merged into deal #N". Both deals must be live: restore an archived one first.

Restoring a merged source makes it visible again, but what the merge moved stays on the deal you
kept. Restoring is not an undo.
