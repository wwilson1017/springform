## The launcher

The launcher is the home page: one tile per app you are allowed to use. Some apps are for admins only, and members never see their tiles.

A tile can show a **badge**, a small count such as "3 due today". Badges refresh about once a minute while the launcher is open.

## Hiding an app (admins)

Go to **Settings → Apps** and switch an app to hidden.

- Members no longer see its tile.
- Admins still see it, marked as hidden, so it can be shown again.
- Hiding does not delete anything and does not switch the app off. Its data stays, and its scheduled jobs keep running.

## Changing the order (admins)

In **Settings → Apps**, move each app up or down with its arrow buttons until the order is right. That order is the same for everyone. Apps you have not placed follow, in their default order.

## Getting a new app

Apps are built for your install by whoever maintains it. To ask for a new app, or a change to one, file a **Request** (see the Requests topic).
