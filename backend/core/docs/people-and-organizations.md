## One directory for every app

**People** and **Organizations** are kept once, in a shared directory. Every app points at the same records, so a customer you add while working in one app is the same customer everywhere else.

A person can belong to several organizations, each with a role (for example "Owner" at one company and "Buyer" at another). One of them can be marked as their main organization.

## What a record holds

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, websites and addresses.
- Tags and a status, for sorting and filtering.
- A **timeline** of notes and changes, newest first. Edits to a record are logged there automatically.
- **Attachments**: files you upload to the record.
- Panels from your apps, such as the deals or orders linked to that person.

## Finding people

Search looks across names, emails, phone numbers and notes. It ignores capital letters and accents, so "jose" finds "José".

## Duplicates

When the same person was entered twice, merge the two records. Their notes, attachments and links from every app move to the record you keep.

## Who can see what

Every member can see the directory. An app may keep some of its own records private (a private to-do, for example); those stay private everywhere, including on a person's page and in the change log.
